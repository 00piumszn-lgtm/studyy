✍️ Grammar Check
Top Grammar Rules
1. Comma Splice: Two sentences with just a comma
❌ "I went to the store, I bought milk."
✓ "I went to the store, and I bought milk."
2. Apostrophe for Possession: Use for ownership, not plurals
❌ "the dog's run fast" (plural)
✓ "the dog's collar" (possession), "the dogs run fast" (plural)
3. its vs it's: Easy mix-up
✓ "it's raining" = it is raining
✓ "the dog lost its collar" = possession
4. Your vs You're:
✓ "you're going" = you are going
✓ "your book" = possession
5. Capital I: Always capitalize when talking about yourself
❌ "i went to school"
✓ "I went to school"
6. Run-on Sentence: Too many ideas, too many "and"s
❌ "I studied hard and I took the test and I got an A."
✓ "I studied hard. Then I took the test and got an A."
7. Fragment: Missing subject or verb
❌ "Because I studied hard." (missing main idea)
✓ "I did well because I studied hard."
8. End Punctuation: Every sentence needs it
❌ "I studied all day"
✓ "I studied all day."
